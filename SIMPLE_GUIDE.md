# 🎯 Super Simple Guide - 5 Steps to Generate

## What You'll See

When you open the workflow editor, you'll now see **LABELS** next to each colored dot telling you what it's for!

## The 5 Steps

### 1️⃣ Add Nodes (Left Sidebar)
Click these icons and drag them to the canvas:
- 📷 **Import** (add 2 of these)
- ⚡ **Generate** (add 1)

### 2️⃣ Upload Images
- Click first Import node → Upload your **style reference** image
- Click second Import node → Upload your **content** image

### 3️⃣ Connect the Dots
Look at the Generate node - you'll see labels:

**TOP DOT (🟠 Orange)** - Says "Reference Image →"
- Drag from first Import's blue dot → to this orange dot

**MIDDLE DOT (🟡 Yellow)** - Says "Source Image →"  
- Drag from second Import's blue dot → to this yellow dot

### 4️⃣ Click "Run Generation"
Big button in the Generate node - can't miss it!

### 5️⃣ Wait for Result
The generated image will appear in the Generate node!

## 🎨 Color Guide (Now Labeled!)

Each dot now has a **visible label** next to it:

| Color | Label Text | What to Connect |
|-------|------------|-----------------|
| 🟠 Orange | "Reference Image →" | Style inspiration image |
| 🟡 Yellow | "Source Image →" | Your content image |
| 🔵 Cyan | "Prompt (optional) →" | Text instructions (skip if you want) |

## 💡 Can't Go Wrong!

The labels tell you exactly what each dot is for. Just:
1. Look at the label
2. Connect the matching image
3. Done!

## Example

**Want to make your photo look like a pro thumbnail?**

1. Upload pro thumbnail → Import #1
2. Upload your photo → Import #2
3. Connect Import #1 → 🟠 "Reference Image →" (top orange)
4. Connect Import #2 → 🟡 "Source Image →" (middle yellow)
5. Click "Run Generation"
6. Magic! ✨

## Still Confused?

Look at the Generate node. You'll literally see text labels:
- **"Reference Image →"** next to the top orange dot
- **"Source Image →"** next to the middle yellow dot
- **"Prompt (optional) →"** next to the bottom cyan dot

Just read the label and connect the right image!

## 🚀 That's It!

The labels make it impossible to mess up. Just follow the text next to each colored dot!
